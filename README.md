# Financial Times Growth Audit

**Live → https://eddieatadapty.github.io/financial-times-flow-rebuild/**

Five ideas to turn more Financial Times users into subscribers, each built into a clickable version of Financial Times's own onboarding. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Use the profile answers you already collect | +10–20% CR to purchase |
| 02 | Put your 4.8 ★ where people decide | +10–15% CR to purchase |
| 03 | Show both plans, and the annual saving in money | +10–15% ARPU (annual mix) · CR +5–15% |
| 04 | Sell the story they tapped, and the $1 month plainly | +5–10% CR to purchase |
| 05 | Offer FT Edit to people who close the plans | +5–10% CR among people who close |

Every price, rating and feature comes from Financial Times's App Store listing, website or in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across 30,000+ subscription apps, not measured lift for Financial Times. They overlap, so they don’t add up.

Static single page, no build step: `python3 -m http.server`.
